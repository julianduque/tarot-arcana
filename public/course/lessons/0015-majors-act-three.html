Majors Act III: The Return (15–21)

By the end of this lesson You'll read the final seven trumps — the Devil through the World — from their full correspondences, completing the Major Arcana. This act moves through shock and illusion into the great lights and, at last, wholeness.

Act III: shattering, illumination, wholeness

The last seven trumps are the superconscious return. The Fool confronts what binds him (the Devil), has his false structures blown apart (the Tower), and is then remade by the great celestial lights — Star, Moon, Sun — before the call of Judgement and the completion of the World. The hardest cards and the most luminous both live here.

Reading all seven from structure

The same move, for the final seven, in order:

XV · The Devil — Ayin (the eye; appearances, the surface of things) + Capricorn (cardinal earth, the goat, worldly ambition) + the path from Tiphareth to Hod. So: bondage to the material and to appearances — the shadow, addiction, the chain. But "eye" cuts both ways: to see the chain is the first step to slipping it. The chains on the card are loose.

XVI · The Tower — Peh (mouth, sudden utterance) + Mars (shock, force) + the path between Netzach and Hod. So: the lightning-word that breaks open what was falsely built — abrupt, Mars-hard, but clearing. Not doom: demolition of the unstable.

XVII · The Star — Tzaddi (fishhook, drawing up from the deep) + Aquarius (the water-bearer, hope for humanity) + the path from Netzach to Yesod. Right after the Tower: healing, faith, the calm after catastrophe — fishing hope back up out of the dark.

XVIII · The Moon — Qoph (the back of the head — the sleeping, animal brain) + Pisces (mutable water, dreams and illusion) + the path from Netzach to Malkuth. So: the unconscious and its tides — dreams, fear, illusion, the uneasy path down into the body; what must be walked through by instinct, not sight.

XIX · The Sun — Resh (the head/face, the shining countenance, consciousness) + the Sun (vitality, clarity) + the path from Hod to Yesod. So: joy, health, truth made plainly visible — the face turned fully to the light after the Moon's night.

XX · Judgement — Shin, a Mother letter, element Fire (the three-tongued flame) + the path from Hod to Malkuth. So: the awakening trumpet, resurrection, the call to a higher life — fire reaching down into dense matter to raise it up. A reckoning that renews.

XXI · The World — Tav (the final letter, the mark or seal) + Saturn (structure, completion, time fulfilled) + the path from Yesod to Malkuth, the last step into the manifest. So: the seal of completion — the journey whole, the dancer at the centre of the cosmos.

See these paths on the lettered Tree in Lesson 8 — Act III is paths 26–32, descending toward Malkuth, the manifest World.

The three Mother-letter trumps, now all seen. The Fool (Aleph / Air), the Hanged Man (Mem / Water), and here Judgement (Shin / Fire) are the three element-trumps. Notice Judgement's Shin = fire: the trumpet, the awakening flame, the resurrection call. The three mothers frame the whole 22.

Practice — you now know all 22

Homework

  1. Lay out all 22 in order (0–21) and walk the whole journey aloud, giving each trump its letter, astrology, and one-line meaning. This is the capstone of the Major Arcana track.
  2. Reframe the "scary" three. For the Devil, the Tower, and the Moon, state the constructive, agency-preserving reading you'd give a nervous querent.
  3. Trace the last paths. Mark paths 26–32 on your Tree; see how the journey descends toward Malkuth, the manifest World.
  4. Read the primary source: Waite on these seven, and — if you have it — DuQuette or Crowley on how Thoth renames Judgement ("Aeon") and the World ("Universe").

Why walk all 22 now: assembling the complete arc in one sitting consolidates three lessons into a single, durable memory of the whole Major Arcana.
