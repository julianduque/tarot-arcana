Majors Act I: The Conscious World (1–7)

By the end of this lesson You'll read the first seven trumps — the Magician through the Chariot — each from its full correspondence stack (Hebrew letter + image, planet or sign, Tree path) plus its core meaning. This is the Fool's Journey and the letters, fused into one reading.

Act I: building a self in the world

The Fool (0, Aleph / Air) sets out and immediately meets the powers of the conscious, outer world — the tools, archetypes, and choices by which a person builds an identity. These seven trumps are the daylight forces: will, intuition, the mother and father, tradition, love, and mastery. Read each now with its full attribution.

Reading all seven from structure

Each trump derives the same way — letter's image + astrological force + what the path bridges. Here are all seven of Act I, in order:

I · The Magician — Beth (house) + Mercury + the path from Kether to Binah. Mercury is skill and communication; the "house" is a vessel for power; the path is the first channel of the crown into form. So: the will focused through skill to manifest — the one who makes the invisible actual.

II · The High Priestess — Gimel (camel) + the Moon + path 13, the long central path from Kether straight down to Tiphareth (across the Abyss). The camel crosses the trackless desert; the Moon rules the tides of intuition; this is the deepest, middle-pillar path. So: the keeper of hidden knowing who carries wisdom across the void — the veil, intuition, what is known but not spoken.

III · The Empress — Daleth (door) + Venus + the path joining Wisdom (Chokmah) and Understanding (Binah). Venus is love and generation; the "door" is the way things come into being; the path unites the two supernal parents. So: the great generative doorway — fertility, abundance, the natural world giving birth.

IV · The Emperor — Heh (window) + Aries + the path from Chokmah to Tiphareth. A window is a framed, ordered aperture; Aries is cardinal fire, initiating force; the path carries Wisdom down toward Beauty. So: the ordering father — structure, authority, force given lawful form.

V · The Hierophant — Vav (nail) + Taurus + the path from Chokmah to Chesed. A nail is what joins and fastens; Taurus is fixed earth, tradition and endurance; the path links Wisdom to Mercy. So: the bridge that binds heaven to earth — teaching, tradition, transmitted meaning, the outer temple to the High Priestess's inner one.

VI · The Lovers — Zayin (sword) + Gemini + the path from Binah to Tiphareth. A sword divides and discriminates; Gemini is the twins, duality; the path joins Understanding to Beauty. So: choice and the union of opposites — discernment in love and values, the cut that decides what you truly want.

VII · The Chariot — Cheth (fence/enclosure) + Cancer + the path from Binah to Geburah. The "fence" is a bounded vehicle; Cancer is the protective shell; the path descends toward severity and force. So: the self as a bounded vehicle driven by will — victory won by holding opposing forces in a single direction.

Homework

  1. Lay out Act I (Magician → Chariot) and, for each, say its letter, astrology, and one-line meaning from memory — check against the table.
  2. Derive, don't recite. Pick two of the seven and build the meaning from letter-image + astrological force, as in the worked examples.
  3. Trace the paths. On your Tree drawing (Lesson 5), mark paths 12–18 — the seven Act I trumps — and see the region of the Tree they occupy.
  4. Read the primary source: Waite's entries (Part III of the Pictorial Key) for these seven; compare his words to your derivations.

Why derive rather than memorize: building each trump from its parts is what makes the whole 22 recallable without a lookup sheet — the mission's real goal.
