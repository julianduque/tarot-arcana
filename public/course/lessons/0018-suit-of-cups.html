The Suit of Cups: Water, Ace to King

By the end of this lesson You'll read the whole suit of Cups as one story — Water flowing from the Ace's open heart to the Ten's fulfilment — and know its four court people. The suit of love, feeling, and the inner life, top to bottom.

Cups = Water (Briah): emotion, love, intuition

Cups are the suit of Water — the World of Briah, creation. Their arena is feeling: love, relationship, intuition, the imagination, the inner life. When Cups fill a reading, the matter is emotional — the heart, not the head or the wallet.

Water's arc runs from the Ace's brimming cup to the Ten's rainbow of fulfilment — but it dips through real grief at the Five and self-deception at the Seven. Feeling, honestly felt, is the whole journey.

The four Cups courts

PageEarth of WaterThe sensitive dreamer; a tender message, an intuitive nudge

The personality of Cups. Deep, flowing, inward. At best: love, empathy, intuition, imagination, connection. At worst: moodiness, escapism, illusion, over-attachment. Cups ask what do you feel — really — and who do you love?

Homework

  1. Walk the suit. Lay all 14 Cups Ace→King and tell the story — the heart opening, dipping into grief at the Five, rising to fulfilment by the Ten.
  2. Derive three pips from number × element + decan; check the titles.
  3. Meet the court. Name someone who fits each of the four Cups courts.
  4. Read the primary source: Waite on the Cups in the Pictorial Key.

Why read the suit as one arc: Cups especially reward feeling the emotional current running Ace→Ten, so any single cup carries the tide it sits in.
