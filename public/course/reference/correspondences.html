Golden Dawn / Hermetic study framework — introductory descriptions
NumberSephirahMeaningStudy question
1KetherCrownSource before differentiation; an Ace offers a beginning, not a completed result.
2ChokmahWisdomOutpouring force; the first polarity. A Two can reveal a relationship or tension.
3BinahUnderstandingForm and limitation make expression possible. Form can sustain or constrain.
4ChesedMercyCoherence, order and provision. Ask when a stable arrangement becomes rigid.
5GeburahSeverityDiscrimination, cutting away and force. Conflict may expose what cannot continue.

9YesodFoundationMediation through images, patterns and imagination. What gives experience a repeatable shape?
10MalkuthKingdomEmbodied manifestation. Results have practical limits and consequences.

This table reports Golden Dawn endpoints only. Justice/Adjustment and Strength/Lust also change numeral between RWS and Thoth. Deviant Moon uses VIII Justice and XI Strength; inspect your own deck rather than assuming RWS numbering.

Elemental dignity matrix
