Master Correspondence Sheet

The whole Hermetic spine on one page, following the Golden Dawn standard (Book T) — the attributions shared, with minor variations, by Rider-Waite-Smith and Thoth. Where Waite or Crowley diverge, it's noted. This is a destination reference: you'll grow into all of it. Print it and keep it by your decks.

The fourfold key. Everything below hangs off one idea: the four letters of the Tetragrammaton יהוה (Yod-Heh-Vav-Heh) = four elements = four suits = four court ranks = four Qabalistic Worlds. Learn this square and the rest is placement.

Note the long-running dispute: the Golden Dawn (and this course) assign Wands = Fire, Swords = Air. Some traditions swap them (Wands = Air). RWS and Thoth both follow the Golden Dawn.

2 · The numbers → the ten sephiroth

Each Minor pip's number is its sephirah on the Tree of Life. The Ace is the pure, undifferentiated root of the element (Kether); Ten is the element fully manifest in the material world (Malkuth).

3 · The court cards → element within element

Each court rank is one of the four elements applied to the suit's element — Tetragrammaton nested inside itself. This is also the great naming trap between decks:

5 · The 36 decans → the Minor pips (2–10)

Each pip 2–10 rules a 10° decan of the zodiac, with a planetary sub-ruler (Chaldean order). The pattern is regular: 2–3–4 = the cardinal sign of the element, 5–6–7 = the fixed sign, 8–9–10 = the mutable sign. The last column gives the Golden Dawn / Thoth card title.
