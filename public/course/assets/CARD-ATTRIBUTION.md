# Card Art Attribution

All images in this directory are from the **Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck**, illustrated
by **Pamela Colman Smith** and published by William Rider & Son, London, **1909**.

- **Status:** Public domain. The deck was published in 1909; the artwork is out of
  copyright in the United States (published before 1929) and in the source country.
- **Source:** [Wikimedia Commons — Category:Rider-Waite tarot deck](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck),
  high-resolution scans (~1100×1920 px).
- **Never invented:** No card art in this course is AI-generated or hand-drawn. Every
  image is a scan of the real 1909 deck.

## Files → source (Wikimedia filename)

**Major Arcana** — `fool.jpg` (`RWS_Tarot_00_Fool.jpg`) plus
`major-01-magician.jpg` … `major-21-world.jpg`, each from the corresponding
`RWS_Tarot_NN_Name.jpg` on Commons (e.g. `major-13-death.jpg` ← `RWS_Tarot_13_Death.jpg`).

**Minor Arcana — complete (all 56).** Every pip and court is present, named
`{rank}-{suit}.jpg` where rank ∈ {ace, two … ten, page, knight, queen, king} and suit ∈
{wands, cups, swords, pentacles}. Sources on Commons: pips `Wands01`–`Wands10` (and
`Cups*`, `Swords*`, `Pents*`); courts `NN11`–`NN14` per suit (11 = Page … 14 = King).
So `queen-cups.jpg` ← `Cups13.jpg`, `six-swords.jpg` ← `Swords06.jpg`, etc. Together with
the 22 Majors above, the full 78-card Rider-Waite-Smith deck is present.

**Court cards** — all 16, as `{page,knight,queen,king}-{wands,cups,swords,pentacles}.jpg`,
from Commons `NN` numbering per suit: 11 = Page, 12 = Knight, 13 = Queen, 14 = King
(e.g. `queen-cups.jpg` ← `Cups13.jpg`, `king-wands.jpg` ← `Wands14.jpg`). Card titles
are printed on the RWS scans, so each rank is self-verifying.
