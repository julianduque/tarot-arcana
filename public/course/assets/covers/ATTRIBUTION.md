# Book Cover Attribution

The 13 book-cover thumbnails in this directory are sourced from the
[Open Library Covers API](https://openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/covers) (Internet Archive),
resolved per title/author and downloaded as medium-resolution JPEGs.

- **Copyright:** Each cover remains the property of its respective publisher. They are shown
  in the course's annotated reading list (`reference/reading-list.html`) for **reference and
  identification only**, the same way a library catalogue or bookstore listing displays a
  cover thumbnail — editorial/nominative use, on an owner-only site.
- **Not public-domain art** (unlike the Rider-Waite-Smith card scans in `../cards/`).
