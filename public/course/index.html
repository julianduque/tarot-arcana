Tarot: The Hermetic Key

Mission Read tarot fluently and truthfully — as a mirror for my own reflection and ethically for others — grounded in the actual system the cards encode (Hermetic Qabalah, astrology, the four elements) rather than memorized keywords. Endpoint: pick up any card in the Rider-Waite-Smith, Thoth, or Deviant Moon deck and read it from structure.

The road ahead

The course climbs from structure to the full Hermetic spine. Each track is built only after the one it rests on. This roadmap is the plan — lessons light up in the list above as they're written.

TrackWhat it builds
FoundationsThe architecture of the 78 cards; the four suits and their elements; numbers and the courts; how to hold and read a card honestly.
Major ArcanaThe 22 trumps as the Fool's journey — each with its Hebrew letter, astrological sign or planet, and Tree-of-Life path.
Minor ArcanaThe 40 pips read from element + number, the 36 zodiacal decans, and the Golden Dawn card titles (Book T).
Court CardsThe 16 courts as element-within-element; personalities, and the classic reading puzzle they pose.
CorrespondencesThe Tree of Life, the four Qabalistic Worlds, elemental dignities — the machinery that makes the whole deck one system.
Reading PracticeSpreads, reversals, ethics, and weaving positions + dignities into one coherent, non-fatalistic narrative.
